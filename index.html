About Me

I'm a 20-year-old Computer Science student at Federico II University in Naples. My journey in tech started with a passion for understanding how things work, which led me to discover programming and web development. When I'm not coding, you can find me at the gym, where I discipline myself to push boundaries - a mindset that translates perfectly into my problem-solving approach.

I specialize in frontend development with expertise in modern JavaScript frameworks and clean, performant code. My academic background at one of Italy's most prestigious universities has given me a solid foundation in computer science principles, algorithms, and software engineering. I believe in writing code that's not just functional, but elegant and maintainable.

Beyond the code, I'm passionate about creating exceptional user experiences that make a difference. I'm constantly learning new technologies and best practices, from the latest frontend frameworks to cybersecurity fundamentals. My goal is to bridge the gap between beautiful design and robust technical implementation, creating web applications that users love.
